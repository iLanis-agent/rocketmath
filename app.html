Rocketmath

The motor casing says "up to 300 feet." Your rocket reads the wind instead. Enter the motor and the airframe; get the simulated apogee, the right ejection delay, the chute size that lands soft, and how far you will walk to get it back.

Flight

-
simulated apogee

Labeled model: vertical point-mass, constant g and air density, fixed Cd, linear propellant burn. Numbers come from the thrust and burn time printed on the motor.

Ejection delay

-
best delay of the set

Ejection should fire at apogee. Firing early or late means the chute opens at speed - that is how shock cords snap. Guidance: standard hobby practice, labeled.

Parachute

-
descent rate under canopy

Terminal velocity from drag equilibrium, Cd 0.8 flat-sheet chute (labeled default). Under ~4.5 m/s lands soft; over ~6 m/s starts breaking fins. Descent time assumes terminal speed the whole way.

Recovery walk

-
downwind drift from the pad

Descent time times wind. Real wind shears with altitude - treat this as the honest minimum walk.

The problem it solves

Every model rocket failure is arithmetic that nobody ran. A motor's average thrust and burn time are printed on the casing; the airframe's mass and diameter are on the spec sheet. From those, the flight is a solvable equation - apogee, coast time, the ejection delay that opens the chute at zero speed instead of forty.

Rocketmath runs the simulation, then prices the recovery: chute diameter against descent rate, wind against your walk back to the pad. Exact where the math is exact, labeled where it is a model.

Honest arithmetic for the three-second silence before the pop. Model assumptions on the label.
